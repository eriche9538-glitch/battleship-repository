# Getting Started with Battleship ⚓

A beginner's guide to playing and mastering Battleship. Learn the basics, understand the game modes, and start building your ability arsenal.

## 👋 Welcome to Battleship!

Battleship is a strategic turn-based naval combat game where you:
1. **Place your fleet** on a hidden 10×10 grid
2. **Attack opponent's grid** to locate and sink their ships
3. **Use special abilities** to gain tactical advantages
4. **Win matches** to earn currency for more powerful abilities
5. **Climb the leaderboard** and prove you're the best captain

## 🎮 Game Basics

### Understanding the Grids

**Your Fleet (Left Grid)**
- Shows your ships in blue
- Enemy shots appear as red hits or white misses
- Your defensive abilities activate here

**Enemy Waters (Right Grid)**
- Starts completely hidden
- Squares reveal as: water (empty), hit (enemy ship), miss (wasted shot)
- Your offensive and recon abilities target here

### Ship Types & Sizes
You start with 5 ships:
- **Battleship**: 5 cells
- **Cruiser**: 4 cells
- **Submarine**: 3 cells
- **Destroyer 1**: 3 cells
- **Destroyer 2**: 2 cells

All ships are destroyed when every cell is hit.

### Taking Turns
1. **Your Turn**: 
   - Fire regular shots or use abilities
   - Can attack different squares or use an ability
   - Confirm your action

2. **Enemy Turn**:
   - Opponent attacks your fleet
   - Watch for hits on your ships
   - Consider defensive abilities if low on health

3. **Victory Condition**:
   - First player to sink all opponent's ships wins
   - Earn currency based on difficulty level

## 🔮 Your First Abilities

When you start, you have **3 free starter abilities**:

### Cross Scan
- Reveal 5 squares in a plus (+) shape
- Use to map safe zones and identify ship clusters
- Cooldown: 2 turns | Uses: 3 per match

**Best Practice**: Use early game to understand board layout

### Ship Scan  
- Confirm and destroy a 3-cell ship you've already found
- High-value ability for securing confirmed kills
- Cooldown: 3 turns | Uses: 2 per match

**Best Practice**: Save for when you've fully mapped a ship position

### Line Sweep
- Reveal 5 consecutive squares (horizontal or vertical)
- Great for systematic board scanning
- Cooldown: 4 turns | Uses: 4 per match

**Best Practice**: Use when you want broad coverage without ability conflicts

## 🎯 Game Modes

### Single Player vs AI

**Easy** 
- AI uses random shot patterns
- Slower response time
- **Reward**: 25 currency per win
- **Best For**: Learning game mechanics

**Medium**
- AI targets hits and searches nearby
- Moderate difficulty
- **Reward**: 100 currency per win
- **Best For**: Practicing strategy

**Master**
- AI hunts aggressively and coordinates attacks
- Very challenging
- **Reward**: 1000 currency per win
- **Best For**: Experienced players

**Tips for AI Play**:
- Start with Easy to understand ship placement patterns
- Progress to Medium once you understand ability combos
- Master will take many wins—focus on learning, not currency

### Multiplayer

**Create a Room**
1. Click "Create Room"
2. Game generates a unique room code (e.g., "SHIP-123")
3. Share code with friends
4. Wait for them to join
5. Click "Start Battle" when ready

**Join a Room**
1. Click "Join Room"
2. Enter friend's room code
3. Click "Join"
4. Wait for room creator to start battle

**Join Random Match**
1. Click "Find Random Opponent"
2. System matches you with similar-skilled player
3. Battle starts immediately

**Multiplayer Tips**:
- Both players must have internet connection
- Battle syncs in real-time
- Cooldowns visible to both players
- Can see opponent's action confirmations

## 🎓 Strategy Tips for Beginners

### Phase 1: Scout First
```
Turns 1-5: Use recon abilities exclusively
- Map the board systematically
- Identify hot zones (ship clusters)
- Plan your attack routes
```

### Phase 2: Apply Pressure
```
Turns 6-15: Mix recon with offense
- Hit isolated high-probability areas
- Use offensive abilities on clusters
- Maintain active ship hunts
```

### Phase 3: Secure Victory
```
Turns 16+: Heavy offense + strategic defense
- Finish off low-health ships
- Use defense if threatened
- Manage remaining cooldowns
```

### Common Beginner Mistakes to Avoid

❌ **Using offensive abilities without recon**
- ✅ Use recon first to locate ships

❌ **Wasting defensive abilities early**
- ✅ Save defense for when truly threatened

❌ **Spreading attacks across the grid**
- ✅ Focus fire on confirmed ship areas

❌ **Ignoring cooldown management**
- ✅ Plan ability rotation to maintain coverage

❌ **Not using starter abilities**
- ✅ All abilities are valuable—use them strategically

## 🆘 Getting Help

### Common Questions

**Q: Why can't I use an ability?**
A: Check the cooldown timer—it must be ready (shows 0)

**Q: Can I undo a move?**
A: No, moves are final once confirmed

**Q: Why did my ability cost fail?**
A: You may not have enough currency. Check your balance.

**Q: How do I change my password?**
A: Use "Forgot Password" on sign-in screen

**Q: Can I reset my account?**
A: Contact support through the help menu

### Tips for Improvement
1. Watch replays of your losses to spot mistakes
2. Try different loadouts and analyze results
3. Play Medium difficulty consistently before Master
4. Study opponent ability usage patterns
5. Join the community Discord for strategy discussions
