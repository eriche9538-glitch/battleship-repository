Battleship Abilities Guide 🎯

Complete reference for all 29 special abilities in Battleship. Abilities are purchased with battle currency and equipped before matches to gain strategic advantages.

📋 Quick Reference by Category
Category	Abilities	Strategy
🔍 Recon	Gather intelligence about enemy positions	Use early to scout and plan attacks
⚔️ Offensive	Strike multiple squares for damage	Maximize hit potential during engagements
🛡️ Defense	Protect your fleet and control pace	Use when threatened or to outmaneuver
☢️ Ultra	Powerful late-game game-changers	Deploy strategically for devastating impact
🔍 RECON ABILITIES

Reconnaissance abilities reveal enemy ship positions and waters. Build intelligence before committing to attacks.

HEXAGONAL OMNISIGHT ⭐ STARTER
Price: Free (starter ability)
Cooldown: 2 turns
Max Uses: 3 per match
Effect: Reveal 5 squares in a cross pattern (center + 4 adjacent)
Best For: Early-game scouting, mapping safe zones
Tip: Use to confirm ship positions before committing offensive abilities
DREADNOUGHT ACQUISITION ⭐ STARTER
Price: Free (starter ability)
Cooldown: 3 turns
Max Uses: 2 per match
Effect: Reveal and instantly sink a confirmed 3-cell enemy ship
Best For: Eliminating medium-sized threats
Tip: Save for when you've located a clear 3-cell ship pattern
HORIZON SEVERANCE ⭐ STARTER
Price: Free (starter ability)
Cooldown: 4 turns
Max Uses: 4 per match
Effect: Reveal 5 consecutive squares in a random horizontal or vertical line
Best For: Efficient area scanning, high-probability coverage
Tip: Useful when you need broad reconnaissance without ability conflicts
NULLSIGNAL
Price: 400 currency
Cooldown: 2 turns
Max Uses: 3 per match
Effect: Reveal 3 squares
Best For: Mid-game ship hunting, finding remaining ships
Tip: Combine with other recon to triangulate ship positions
ABYSSAL ECHO
Price: 650 currency
Cooldown: 6 turns
Max Uses: 1 per match
Effect: Scan a 3×3 ring around a random target area
Best For: Dense ship concentration scanning, safe area mapping
Tip: Great for identifying "no ship" zones to focus fire elsewhere
CELESTIAL EYE
Price: 900 currency
Cooldown: 10 turns
Max Uses: 4 per match
Effect: Reveal a hidden ship square and all nearby water squares
Best For: Pinpointing exact ship locations and their perimeters
Tip: After using, attack the revealed neighbors for high efficiency
THERMAL DOMINION
Price: 750 currency
Cooldown: 5 turns
Max Uses: 1 per match
Effect: Scan a 3×3 area of waters
Best For: Methodical grid scanning, medium-density ship detection
Tip: Use in sequence to systematically map the board
PREY ACQUIRED
Price: 1000 currency
Cooldown: 5 turns
Max Uses: 1 per match
Effect: Track and sink a remaining 3-tile ship
Best For: Eliminating the last medium ship, securing victory
Tip: Saves time in endgame when hunting specific ship sizes
⚔️ OFFENSIVE ABILITIES

Offensive abilities deal damage across multiple squares, eliminate ships, and apply pressure.

HEAVEN'S WRATH
Price: 750 currency
Cooldown: 15 turns
Max Uses: 3 per match
Effect: Bomb 3 random enemy squares
Best For: Early harassment, maintaining offensive pressure
Tip: Great when you have partial ship intel; randomness can yield lucky hits
HELLFALL
Price: 1000 currency
Cooldown: 5 turns
Max Uses: 1 per match
Effect: Launch 7 missiles at random squares
Best For: Heavy attack phase, punishing clustered fleets
Tip: High volume increases odds of hitting multiple ships
VOIDPIERCER
Price: 850 currency
Cooldown: 12 turns
Max Uses: Unlimited per match
Effect: Fire through 5 consecutive squares in a straight line
Best For: Eliminating ship lines, hitting aligned targets
Tip: Coordinate with Recon to predict ship alignment
DIVINE CROSSHAIR
Price: 1200 currency
Cooldown: 15 turns
Max Uses: 1 per match
Effect: Strike 1 cross patterns at once (5 squares total)
Best For: Double-pattern assault, decimating fleets
Tip: Devastating when paired with Heat Map reconnaissance
THOUSAND-FOLD
Price: 900 currency
Cooldown: 9 turns
Max Uses: 1 per match
Effect: Scatter 8 shots across enemy waters
Best For: Coverage attack, high-volume strikes
Tip: Best used after narrowing down ship locations
ABYSSAL SPEAR
Price: 1500 currency
Cooldown: 10 turns
Max Uses: 1 per match
Effect: Torpedo a complete random row or column
Best For: Large-scale devastation, eliminating entire ship formations
Tip: Can wipe 3-5 ships in a single use if lucky
🛡️ DEFENSE ABILITIES

Defensive abilities protect your fleet, mitigate damage, and control the pace of battle.

PHANTOM VEIL
Price: 600 currency
Cooldown: 4 turns
Max Uses: 1 per match
Effect: Hide your fleet and skip the enemy turn
Best For: Dodging incoming attacks, regaining initiative
Tip: Use when low on ship health to avoid damage and reset board state
FALSE ADMIRAL
Price: 1100 currency
Cooldown: 4 turns
Max Uses: 2 per match
Effect: Deploy a decoy that absorbs the next enemy shot
Best For: Protecting critical ships, tricking AI targeting
Tip: Deploy before enemy turn; decoy takes first hit
TITANFORGE
Price: 1300 currency
Cooldown: 5 turns
Max Uses: 3 per match
Effect: Protect your fleet from the next enemy hit
Best For: Preventing knockout blows, strategic damage mitigation
Tip: Combine with Decoy Ship for a 2-hit buffer
SECOND ASCENSION
Price: 1200 currency
Cooldown: 5 turns
Max Uses: 1 per match
Effect: Repair one damaged ship tile and skip the enemy turn
Best For: Healing critical ships, extending survival
Tip: Save your largest ships; skipping enemy turn is bonus defensive value
GRAVEWATER
Price: 1500 currency
Cooldown: 7 turns
Max Uses: 2 per match
Effect: Seed 5 mines across random enemy waters
Best For: Offensive defense, punishing enemy scouting
Tip: Mines detonate when enemy attacks those squares, dealing bonus damage
AEGIS PRIME
Price: 1800 currency
Cooldown: 5 turns
Max Uses: 5 per match
Effect: Block the next two enemy shots
Best For: Critical defense moments, weathering heavy barrages
Tip: Deploy before opponent's big offensive turn

Best For: Endgame ship hunting, final scouting push
Tip: Massive intelligence gain; use when closing in on victory
LEGION OF THE LOST
Price: 150,000 currency
Cooldown: 8 turns
Max Uses: 1 per match
Effect: Reveal 12 random enemy squares and evade fire
Best For: Complete game reversal, extreme evasion + recon
Tip: Most effective in 1v1 endgame scenarios
LAST JUDGEMENT
Price: 200,000 currency
Cooldown: 5 turns
Max Uses: 3 per match
Effect: Fire a devastating salvo across a complete random row
Best For: Finishing blow, eliminating remaining ships in one attack
Tip: Can potentially eliminate 2-3 ships simultaneously
CATACLYSM PROTOCOL
Price: 300,000 currency
Cooldown: 8 turns
Max Uses: 2 per match
Effect: Reveal 5 enemy ship tiles instantly
Best For: Precise targeting in endgame, sinking final ships
Tip: Highest cost ability; save for absolutely critical moments
💡 Strategy Guide
Early Game (Turns 1-10)
Use HEXAGONAL OMNISIGHT & HORIZON SEVERANCE to map board
Identify ship clustering patterns
Save DREADNOUGHT ACQUISITION for confirmed targets
Light harassment with HEAVEN'S WRATH
Maintain recon ability cooldowns
Mid Game (Turns 11-20)
Transition to NULLSIGNAL & THERMAL DOMINION for precision
Deploy offensive abilities (HELLFALL, DIVINE CROSSHAIR)
Use defense (PHANTOM VEIL, FALSE ADMIRAL) when needed
Hunt mid-sized ships with targeted abilities
Manage cooldowns carefully
Late Game (Turns 20+)
Use Ultra Abilities strategically for game-winning momentum
Deploy PREY ACQUIRED to eliminate last 3-cell ships
Use ABYSSAL SPEAR for final area sweeps
Combine VOIDPIERCER with known alignments
Save SECOND ASCENSION for critical moments
Use AEGIS PRIME for final defensive stand

❓ FAQ

Q: Can I change abilities between matches?

A: Yes! Create different loadouts and select before each match.

Q: Do cooldowns carry between matches?

A: No, cooldowns reset at the start of each new match.

Q: Can I use unlimited ability uses?

A: Some abilities have max uses per match (usually 2-4), others are unlimited.

Q: How many abilities can I equip?

A: Loadout slots expand with progression; typically 6-10 slots available.

Q: Are Ultra abilities worth 35000+ currency?

A: They're powerful but take time to earn. Build a balanced arsenal first.

Q: Can I refund ability purchases?

A: No, but you earn currency quickly through wins.
